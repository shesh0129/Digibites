Food Allergy Emergency Guidelines

If a student may be having an allergic reaction:

  1. Stop eating and tell a canteen staff member and a trusted adult immediately. Stay with the student.
  2. For trouble breathing or swallowing, throat or tongue swelling, fainting, or rapidly worsening symptoms, call emergency services now.
  3. If the student has a prescribed epinephrine auto-injector, help them use it according to its instructions. Do not delay the emergency call.
  4. Tell responders what the student ate and any known allergies. Urgent medical care is still needed even if symptoms improve.
Call 911 for a severe reaction

This guide does not replace emergency responders or a healthcare professional.
